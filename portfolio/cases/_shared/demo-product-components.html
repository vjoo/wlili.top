03 · INDUSTRY · Industry

Redesigned verification flow for 6 industries

From counter service to construction attendance, every form factor and workflow has been field-tested.

01 GOVERNMENT

Service Hall · Instant Verify

Desktop kiosk at the service counter. Citizens scan ID + face match, complete verification in 30 seconds, auto-sync to government backend.

02 CAMPUS

Dormitory · Auto Check-in

Wall-mounted A81A at dorm entrances. Students face-scan to enter, auto-sync check-in/out to student affairs. Abnormal patterns alert counselors within 5 min.

03 PARK

Park Access · Frictionless

Visitors book via mini-program, scan QR + face at A81A kiosk, gates auto-open and notify host. Real-time sync to property management system.

04 CONSTRUCTION

Site Attendance · Live Check-in

MA55A at site gate. Workers scan ID + face for attendance, auto-generate real-name ledger uploaded to housing authority. IP68 rugged for harsh environments.

05 HOTEL

Hospitality · Self Check-in

Front-desk kiosk completes ID matching and dispenses room key automatically. No front-desk queue. Integrates with public security hotel registration system.

06 HEALTHCARE

Hospital · One-ID Access

A81A at clinic entrance handles e-health card verification, appointment confirm, and insurance binding. Patients skip repeated window queues. Data feeds back to insurance bureau.
